# Features

<!-- GA-GENERATED:index -->

- [Account deletion](account-deletion.md) - A person deletes their account from the profile page, or Clerk reports it deleted; the person is anonymised and their shared expenses stay.
- [Activity feed](activity-feed.md) - A paged list of what happened in the caller's groups: expenses, settlements, members and group changes.
- [Balances and totals](balances.md) - Show who owes whom in a group, simplified to the fewest payments, and how much the group and the caller have spent.
- [Biometric lock](biometric-lock.md) - A person can register a passkey device; the signed-in app then asks for it once per browser session before showing anything.
- [Expenses](expenses.md) - Record what someone paid in a group and how it is split, see each expense's breakdown, edit its description, or delete it.
- [Feedback](feedback.md) - A signed-in person can send one piece of feedback, a title and a description.
- [Friends and contacts](friends-and-contacts.md) - Find a signed-up person by email and add them as a friend, or add someone who hasn't signed up as an offline contact.
- [Groups](groups.md) - Create a group, see its expenses, balances and totals, edit it, add members, and delete it.
- [Push notifications](notifications.md) - Members get a OneSignal push when an expense, settlement, group or membership changes; the profile page lets a person allow them.
- [Privacy policy and deletion page](privacy-policy.md) - Public /privacy and /delete-account pages that Google Play's listing points at, plus the asset links file for the Android app.
- [Reports](reports.md) - Download a group's expenses as an Excel workbook or a text file from the group screen.
- [Settle up](settle-up.md) - Record that one member paid another back, from a balance line, as a settlement expense.
